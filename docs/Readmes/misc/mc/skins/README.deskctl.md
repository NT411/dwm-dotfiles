Deskctl Midnight Commander skins

Nine 256-color accent skins match the accents selected by deskctl.
Applying or restoring an accent in deskctl selects its matching MC skin.
Reopen MC after changing the accent.

Preview a skin with:

    mc --skin deskctl-accent-green

Installed skins:
- deskctl-accent-blue
- deskctl-accent-brown
- deskctl-accent-green
- deskctl-accent-orange
- deskctl-accent-pink
- deskctl-accent-purple
- deskctl-accent-red
- deskctl-accent-white
- deskctl-accent-yellow
